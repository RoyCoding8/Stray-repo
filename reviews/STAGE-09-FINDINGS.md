**What this file is.** A single table of every finding raised in Stage 9, each row
carrying reproduction, impact, owner, fix and recheck where those are known.

**What this file is not, and the earlier title overclaimed all three.** It is not a
finding that every row was re-verified at the source. Only rows whose recheck column
names a source check were; the rest are inherited from a lane report and are marked
as such. It is not a work queue: owner is unstated for most rows, and a row with no
owner is not assigned work. It is not a verified-location index either. The pass at
`74261ef` re-checked every cited `file:line` in the settlement and ad01 sources and
found seven wrong, listed in the last section; **the pass at `693575e` re-checked those
seven again, rewrote every `location` cell it touched, and re-verified the presence of
the symbol behind every non-OPEN row.** The table of seven is kept rather than deleted,
and each entry carries a second verdict.

**How to read a status.** `REPAIRED` means a recheck proved the defect's behaviour is
gone, and the recheck cell says what proved it. `PARTIAL` means part of it is gone and
the rest is named. `OPEN` means no recheck has closed it. `CONFIRMED` means the row's
own claim was re-read and holds, and is not a repair. `SUPERSEDED` means a later id
replaced it, and the row is kept for the reasoning with the successor named. `RETRACTED`
means the finding's own number was withdrawn and the row is kept to stop it being cited.
`RESOLVED` means the subject was a result rather than a defect. Nothing here is dropped
for being obsolete. **A status cell that names no source check is a claim, not a
measurement, and the recheck cell says which of the two it is.**
