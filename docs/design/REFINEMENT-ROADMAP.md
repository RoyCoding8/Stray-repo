## The current decision

**The 2026-10-01 consolidation batch (`codex/stage09-consolidation-2026-10-01`, first integrated at `8a207ab`, reviewed and repaired at `8cc1927`, standing at `5b7f1ca`) returned four negatives and one non-establishment. The mechanism was confirmed, then overturned to *too generous* by an independent review, and both halves of that over-claim have since been repaired.** The six dispositions are the acceptance pass's own and are recorded in full in the [project ledger](../../reports/PROJECT-LEDGER.md#the-consolidation-batch-six-dispositions-none-softened). The short form, kept separate deliberately:

| Mechanism | Acquisition | Utility | Transfer | Autonomous selection | Learner improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CONFIRMED as to the durable chain; the freeze over-claimed, repaired, and its causal link unbound until X4b** | NEGATIVE | NEGATIVE, not measurable | NEGATIVE | NOT ESTABLISHED | NEGATIVE |

What the batch built is real and worth keeping: durable dispatch identity, authority as a mandatory type rather than a runtime branch, a freeze enforced against the parse rather than a hand-kept list, and one mission owner. Two of those four claims were over-claimed when first written. The freeze matched two node types and admitted a write made through a mapping method, and the runtime comparison named as the layer that catches this cannot fire, because a revision's bytes run out of process against a view carrying none of the six frozen fields. X3 rebuilt the check on the parse's own binding contexts and deleted the helper whose docstring promised a backstop. The effect identity was separately minted as a formatted string that named no row, so two of the chain's seven arrows were bound by position rather than by cause; X4b made the identity a value read back and checked against the database before it is written. Five live concurrency and parse defects are now fixed that no lane's own gate could see — four from two search passes, the fifth from the review. **None of that is evidence of learning.** The mechanism's durable chain is confirmed; the utility question is unanswered because its input does not exist, and the two must not be read as one result.

The binding constraint is not the one this document previously named. **The output budget is not it, and this claim was falsified after it was written.** Acquisition returned 0 of 4 lineages, but the denominator is one returned artifact, not a rate: of five sends, one timed out and three returned HTTP 502. 7000 is a constant in B12's driver rather than a loader rule, and a policy that repairs 3 of 9 dev instances fits the served 2048-token budget with room to spare. B17 added two more sends, and **3 of 3 responses were prose, all truncated at the budget, none a policy.** The cause is prose-not-code, and it is a route-and-protocol question rather than an open research question about the design. The acquisition disposition stands; the explanation for it has changed.

The real constraint is that **the system has no independent unit for any learning claim.** Every disposition above acquisition is a two-arm contrast, and the design supplies one independent unit of variation per structure, so a contrast is n=1 however many rows it runs. The Boolean structure offers one hypothesis class against six required, and the class is a published constant that all 72 tasks carry by construction, so no panel choice over the existing tasks can move it. The SWE side reaches its threshold only by counting dev and held-out together, which is 3 and 6 per split against six required — clearing the bar through the census the split exists to prevent. A two-domain demonstration is not a two-domain result, and going from one unit to six needs six different generators.

Beneath all of it, the earlier work stands as recorded. The [project inventory](../../reports/PROJECT-INVENTORY.md) reconciles source and raw evidence at `6096442` and defines fifteen capability areas with code, seven foundation and eight cognitive. That is an inventory, not a completion percentage. E1 r2/r3 yielded no usable Boolean program; E2 has a contrast on a panel without positive headroom; E3 is an authored-policy versus schedule comparison; E4 received six executable candidates but admitted no eligible revision. The 2026-10-01 batch ran the same questions on stronger plumbing and reached the same four answers, which is itself worth stating plainly: **better mechanism did not produce better outcomes.**

Stage 9 remains active until acquisition, transfer, autonomous selection and learner improvement are answered on their own terms. A valid null can close a question; four of these are nulls, and a null on acquisition closes the acquisition question while leaving the three downstream ones unmeasured rather than answered. Stage 10 requires a connected general prototype with evidence for its selected scope, and a mission entry that crossed two task structures under a scripted order is not yet that. Stage 11 requires deployment qualification; stage 12 requires a supported release. None follows from a green instrument, from running four named experiments, or from a confirmed mechanism.
