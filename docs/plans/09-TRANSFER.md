# P09. Representations and transfer

Reuse acquired computation on meaningfully changed work. Depends on [P02](02-MEMORY.md), [P03](03-CONTEXT.md) and [P04](04-CAPABILITIES.md). The [master plan](MASTER-PLAN.md) owns common rules. This part may begin before teams or learner revision.

## Transfer contract

A retained representation or abstraction declares the source structure it accepts, what its transformation preserves, the operations it enables, dependencies and known counterexamples. Its contract may have a formal proof, empirical scope or both. Do not equate empirical support with a universal theorem.

Separate exact reuse, adaptation and new construction. If an adapter is generated, record its bytes and costs separately from the reused core. The executed dependency closure identifies both. A task name or domain label is not evidence that the core applies.

Start from recurring computations in the actual experience corpus. Choose a transfer target with different inputs, composition or domain assumptions. The target's public obligation and independent checker must remain meaningful without the proposed representation.

Compare a structured text lesson, direct task solving and executable reuse. A human-provided common wrapper or adapter is supplied structure. It cannot establish that the system discovered the bridge between domains.

## Delivery slices

1. **P09-1. Select a real reuse opportunity.** Inspect acquired procedures and their use records. State the shared structure and one counterexample. If none exists, report the corpus limitation and acquire relevant experience rather than invent a transfer claim.
2. **P09-2. Construct and check an abstraction.** Build the representation or adapter from development evidence. Test preservation and applicability through P04's real invocation path.
3. **P09-3. Use it on held-out changed work.** Freeze the core and record any adaptation. Reload in a fresh process and compare the resulting computation and cost with the baselines.

## Acceptance scenarios

- **P09-A.** The retained core executes on changed work and contributes an observable intermediate or final result. A stored answer cannot satisfy this scenario.
- **P09-B.** The declared preservation property fails on a counterexample. Narrow or reject applicability instead of relabeling the failure.
- **P09-C.** Replace the core with a no-op. A meaningful check detects the lost behavior.
- **P09-D.** Adaptation reconstructs the entire task solution. Report new construction rather than cheap core reuse.
- **P09-E.** A claimed cross-domain bridge is supplied by the evaluator or authored adapter. Label the supplied structure and limit the transfer conclusion.

Live acquisition and changed-task use are distinct from a positive transfer benefit. Measure both adaptation and original acquisition costs. Begin with software and independently checkable computational mathematics, without requiring the same reduction algorithm in both.

## Working checkpoint

The system can qualify and execute a reusable representation with explicit transfer scope. General cross-domain invention remains a broader objective. Do not build a universal ontology or symbolic synthesizer without a corpus supporting that choice. See the older [representation design](../design/REPRESENTATION-AND-TRANSFER.md) for hypotheses to reassess.
