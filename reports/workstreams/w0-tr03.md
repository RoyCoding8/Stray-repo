## Verdict

**CONFIRMED, and worse than the review states.**

The reviewer's structural claim is correct on every number I measured. Two of
its claims are incomplete, and the incompleteness is the more serious half of
the finding:

- `build_step_lineages` produces four *distinct* programs, not one. The review

## The three specific claims, checked one at a time

**"Four AST records, one unique AST entry program, `earned == false` for all

differentiator. Line 187 reads:

and the returned `{"policy_id": ..., "entry": _if(...)}` never mentions either
name. `_VARIANTS` is a four-entry table of `("case-01", "code.localize")`-style

whose records hash alike would be caught as one lineage wearing four names",
which is the exact failure mode present, and the seam that was supposed to

**"Four graph records, one unique graph node program."** CONFIRMED, with an

**"The four STEP origins are all authored-control."** CONFIRMED. All four read
`record["artifact"]["origin"] == "authored-control"`, which is in

None of the four carries an `acquisition_evidence` key at all, so a reader
checking the top level sees `None` and cannot distinguish "never acquired" from
"the field is not part of this cell's shape". That inconsistency is itself a
finding, and it is a live trap for W1: the natural audit

CONFIRMED, at four sites.

- Lines 88-91: the `LINEAGES_PER_CELL = 4` comment. `"independent" is the

the digest alone, and a digest is a statement about bytes, not about process.

## Why digest-as-lineage is wrong in both directions

**False positive (the measured case).** Four records, one program, four
digests. The digest test passes because `policy_id` and `applicability` are
inside the hashed record. `applicability={"world": 2, "arm": "E1", "lineage":
index}` is written by `build_step_lineages` itself, so the builder
manufactures the digest difference it then cites as proof of independence. The
`source_digest` inside the STEP artifact is a *content* hash of the source, so
for STEP the record digest is redundant with the program, and for AST/graph it
is the `policy_id` all over again.

**False negative (the review's second point, and the reason digest cannot be
repaired by inverting it).** Two genuinely independent live calls that converge
on the same program produce the same digest. Requiring digest distinctness to
count a lineage would silently drop real replicates and bias acquisition
sampling toward the model's novelty rather than its reliability. A definition
that both over-counts fixtures and under-counts convergent live acquisitions is
not a weak definition, it is the wrong axis.

## Corrected definition for W1/E1

Adopt this before any live campaign runs. One definition, not a pair.

**A lineage is one construction opportunity that produced one policy record.
Lineages are counted, never deduplicated. Two lineages are behaviourally
distinct if their executed action traces over the panel differ, and
behavioural distinctness is a reported property of a lineage set, not a
admission criterion for entering it.**

Three clauses, each doing one job.

1. **The unit is the construction opportunity, not the record.** Every call to
   a construction path that asks a model to write a policy is one opportunity,
   whether or not the resulting bytes match another call's bytes. A lineage is
   that opportunity plus its record. This satisfies constraint (b): convergent
   independent calls each count, because they each had their own opportunity.
2. **Opportunities are independent when they share no lineage or feedback
   history.** Two opportunities are independent iff their ancestor chains are
   disjoint, i.e. neither descends from the other's construction record, and
   neither was given the other's output, digest or feedback as input. This
   satisfies constraint (a). Note what this deliberately permits: two
   opportunities issued from the same prompt with the same seed. Nothing is
   shared, so they are independent, and their programs may be identical.
3. **Behavioural duplication is reported, never enforced.** Compute the
   behaviour signature per lineage and publish the collision count. A campaign
   that requested 4 lineages and got 4 opportunities reports 4 lineages plus
   "1 distinct behaviour". It does not report 1 lineage, and it does not reject
   a duplicate as non-independent.

The counting rule, stated once:

> `lineages_run = count(construction_opportunity)`,
> `distinct_behaviours = count(distinct(behaviour_signature))`,
> and the pair is always published together.

The third clause is what replaces the digest test. Under the current code a
duplicate record silently inflates `lineages_run` with no accompanying
`distinct_behaviours` figure, which is the whole failure.

### Fields that must be persisted per construction opportunity

Minimal set, and where it already exists.

| Field | Purpose | Exists today? |
|---|---|---|
| `origin` | `authored-control` / `fixture-stand-in` / `model-acquired` vs the live package's `acquired` | **Yes.** `policy_step.POLICY_ORIGINS`, `frontier.PACKAGE_ORIGINS`, and the `artifact.origin` on every STEP record. |
| `acquisition_evidence.earned` | whether a live provider actually produced the bytes | **Partly.** Present on the AST cell only. Absent on STEP and graph. |
| `acquisition_evidence.operation_id` | the one dispatch that produced these bytes; the natural opportunity id | **Yes, on the live path.** `live_construct.read_acquisition_evidence` returns it, and `frontier.validate_acquisition_evidence` requires a non-empty `operation_id` in provenance. |
| `acquisition_evidence.prompt_digest` | what the model was asked. Two opportunities with equal prompt digests that received no shared history are still independent | **Yes.** Both live paths compute it. |
| `acquisition_evidence.receipt_identity` + `response_digest` | the settled provider response, the ground truth for "these are the bytes a provider wrote" | **Yes.** Returned by `read_acquisition_evidence`, required by `validate_acquisition_evidence`. |
| `acquisition_evidence.route` + `simulated` + `requested_model` | the anti-double check the live path already implements | **Yes.** `earned` is false unless the adapter is `http`, the route metadata is complete, the model resolves, and `simulated` is absent. |
| `parent_digest` | the lineage edge. Independence is disjointness of ancestor chains | **Yes.** On every STEP artifact (`make_policy_artifact` takes it) and in `frontier.REVISION_IDENTITY_FIELDS`. |
| `acquisition_round` / `round` | separates a re-rolled round from a genuine re-construction at the same depth | **Yes.** `frontier.REVISION_IDENTITY_FIELDS` and `ROUND_RESULT_FIELDS`. |
| **`construction_opportunity_id`** | the explicit id of one construction attempt, distinct from the artifact it produced | **NO. This is the one genuinely missing field.** |
| **`feedback_inputs`** | the digests of any parent output, repair feedback or critique fed into this construction | **NO. This is the second missing field.** |
| **`behaviour_signature`** | the executed-trace fingerprint that makes clause 3 checkable | **NO. This is the third missing field.** |

Three additions, and the honest news is that two of the three are nearly free.
`construction_opportunity_id` is `operation_id` in the live path and the
`(cell, index)` pair in the fixture path, so the live machinery already has the
identity and the fixture builders already have the index.
`feedback_inputs` is derivable from `parent_digest` plus the frontier's
ancestor walk, so it can start as a derived value rather than a new persisted
field. `behaviour_signature` is the one real addition: the experiment already
computes full action traces per row and already has `_intern` at line 1673 for
fingerprinting values, so this is a hash over data the run already holds, not a
new capture.

### The invariant that makes this checkable

Any lineage whose `origin` is not `acquired`/`model-acquired` and whose
`acquisition_evidence.earned` is not `true` is a **control**. Controls are
counted, run and reported, and they never contribute to a count of requested
*live* lineages. W1 must therefore track two integers per cell, not one:
`lineages_run` and `live_lineages`, and every report must show both.

## Retention guidance for the existing fixtures

Retain all twelve. They are not defects; they are the correct measurement of
what the representations can express, and deleting them would throw away real
evidence.

- **The AST cell's real value is its refusal.** `s09_swe_ast.missing_cells()`
  records that the frozen `_VIEW_TYPES` publishes no field for the program
  under repair and no node builds replacement source text. That is a genuine
  representation-world binding finding, and it is attached to every lineage
  already. Four records do not damage it. One would not improve it.
- **The graph cell's real value is that it is a real executor over a real
  `World`.** `ordering_graph_policy.World` being a value rather than a
  subclass is what makes the SWE world a binding instead of a re-shape, and
  `load_policy` admitting the record with the guard decided by the real
  `_evaluate_guard` on 30 of 30 held-out instances is a measured
  qualification result. One record would establish exactly as much as four.
- **The STEP cell is the only one with four distinct programs**, and they are
  a legitimate authored behavioural control: it demonstrates that search order
  is a real, expressible seam. Its honest current value is that it also
  measures the seam as inert on this panel.

The rule for how they must not be counted. These twelve are controls. They
qualify the representations and bound what the panel can express. They are
evidence about the *instrument*, not about *acquisition*, and they contribute
zero to any requested live lineage count. `LINEAGES_PER_CELL = 4` describes how
many control records a cell ships; it must never be read as "four lineages were
acquired", and the fix is not to change the number but to stop letting the
number travel under the word "independent".

One structural note that is larger than this lane. The live acquisition path in
`live_construct.py` and `frontier.py` already implements a strict, well-tested
notion of earned acquisition: a committed prompt, a settled success receipt,
http-adapter route metadata, resolved model, no `simulated` flag, and a
response digest that must match the member's bytes. TR-03's problem is
confined to the **fixture experiment module**, which re-derives a weaker notion
of lineage from scratch instead of using that machinery. The lowest-churn fix
may be to have the fixture cells report in the same vocabulary the live path
already enforces, rather than to add a new concept to `frontier.py`.
