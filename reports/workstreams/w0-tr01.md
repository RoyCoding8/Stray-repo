## 1. The real exposure authority does not subtract. It carries separately

`D:/AI/Agent-Society-v2/.worktrees/w0-tr01/experiments/ad01/s09_exposure_ledger.py:790-813`

## 5. What the handover actually got wrong

The closing rationale in `reports/STAGE-09-HANDOVER.md:256-266` rests on two
load-bearing claims. The first is false and the second is real.

**False: that the 5563 is unreconcilable.** §5.1 calls the units "unsettled and
unreconcilable" and reasons that a fresh run "would add unmeasurable exposure to
a hole that cannot be closed." But the units *were* reconciled. They are
VERIFIED, recomputed from durable rows, and the ledger states the state
explicitly. What they are is *unsettled*. The review conflated those. The
hedge "and unreconcilable" is doing the work the evidence does not support, and
`reports/evidence/invl02-live/store-reconciliation.json:43` says the opposite in
almost those words: "the exposure is now VERIFIED as a figure and remains
UNRESOLVED as a debt. Verifying the number does not discharge it."

**Real: that a new run would add unmeasurable exposure.** This survives. No
receipt on this free route has ever reported a charge, and adding a run adds
units whose cost cannot be measured. This is a genuine reason to be careful
about dispatch volume. It is not a reason to refuse a finite campaign forever.

**The decision the handover actually made, it made on a superseded authority.**
§5.1 cites `reports/PROJECT-LEDGER.md:110` as the rule forbidding new live calls
before earlier exposure is reconciled. That file is now **62 lines**. Line 110
does not exist and never did at this revision. The 1,614-line ledger it cited
was replaced by the consolidation in `dfbd557`, one commit before this
revision, and the replacement says something different. At
`reports/PROJECT-LEDGER.md:36` the current ledger states: "Historical uncertain
reservations preserved. Fresh finite free-route allocations are authorized
separately; no assumption of settlement or measured billing."

So the current authority explicitly authorises the thing §5.1 refused. The
handover's rationale is not merely contested by the review, it cites a rule
that no longer exists in the document that now owns the question. The review
identified a contradiction with M1. The stronger finding is a contradiction with
the *current* ledger, which supersedes both.

## Principles applied

`launch_plan` in my own worktree and confirming the import path first. The
