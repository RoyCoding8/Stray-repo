## Root cause

StepPolicyConsumer rebuilt remaining model allowance from the packet on
every iteration, wiping the in-loop decrement. Private STEP state
restarted at {} on every decide. Exhausted and step-failed paths
returned without a durable row. Failures dropped their operation
identity. A format-string defect in the durable count helper hid the
allowance (unsupported format character on LIKE patterns, caught as 0).

## Invariant restored

Private policy state persists across boundaries from the durable
s09_policy_state final of the prior sequence. Cumulative policy steps
and model calls enforce at most 6 per episode from durable operation
counts, including construction, repair, empty output, and provider
failures. Pending STEP action identity exists as a broker operation
before dispatch effects. Resume reuses the same deterministic operation
and settled text, never redraws model bytes. Exhausted and refused
paths persist as incorporated terminal rows. Settled receipts stay
attributable by operation identity even when text decoding falls back
to empty.
