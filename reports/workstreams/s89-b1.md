Base: `20dc163`. Branch here is `wt/s89-b1` (`wt/s89-b1-grounding` does not exist locally).
Scope: read-only grounding for STAGE-09-CONSOLIDATION-01 section B1.

## 1. Path map, both domains

Entry is the `ad01-traj` CLI (`experiments/ad01/cli.py:85`).
Subcommands map to public functions. `run` calls `trajectory.run_campaign`
(`cli.py:236`). `resume` calls `trajectory.resume_campaign` (`cli.py:250`).
`use` calls `trajectory.run_use` (`cli.py:206`). `export` calls
`records.export_campaign` (`cli.py:152`). `replay` calls
`records.load_export` plus `doubles.replay_export` (`cli.py:166-168`).
`recompute` calls `records.recompute_accounting` (`cli.py:183`).

The two domains share one driver. The split happens at family dispatch
points listed per stage below.

### Authoritative state

Postgres holds the truth. `trajectory.ensure_campaign` (`trajectory.py:136`)
records the task schedule in `command_journal`, binds study authority through
`settlement.authority.bind_study`, and admits a commitment through
`store.admit_commitment`. Per-boundary records land in `attempts` plus
`attempt_observations` with `kind` of `decision`, `correction`, or `boundary`
(`trajectory.record_decision:183`, `trajectory._publish_boundary:555`).
Broker operations and receipts live in `operations` and `receipts`
(`broker.ensure_operation:244`, `broker.dispatch_operation:410`).
Diagnostic checkpoints land in `study_phases`
(`trajectory._note_diagnostic_checkpoint:265`). Both domains use identical
tables. No domain-specific state store exists.

### Effect admission

Admission has two layers. The broker layer validates effect payloads
(`broker.validate_effect:54`) and prepares reservations
(`store.prepare_operation`, called from `broker.ensure_operation:244`).
AD01 uses two effects. `model-inference` carries learner and construction
calls (`learner.model_propose:147`, `construct._call:81`). `sandbox-exec`
carries member execution (`method_exec.run_member_out_of_process:172`,
ensured at `method_exec.py:214`, dispatched at `:261`).
The investigation layer is `DecisionConsumer.decide`
(`agenda_policy.py:157`). It wraps a proposer and `admit_investigation`
(`trajectory.py:1129`). Admission refuses malformed proposals, invented basis
references, protected-use smuggling, and off-curriculum or off-world targets.
Both domains pass the same gate. The gate has exactly one production
construction site (`trajectory._run_boundary:346`).

### Model-visible context

`packet.decision_packet` (`packet.py:58`) builds the propose/admit context.
`learner.model_propose` (`learner.py:159`) renders it after
`LEARNER_INSTRUCTION` into one broker `model-inference` operation with a
deterministic id (`ad01-<cid>-learner-<seq>[-c<attempt>]`, `learner.py:127`).
`packet.construction_packet` (`packet.py:139`) plus
`render_construction_prompt` (`packet.py:173`) builds the construction
context. `construct._prompt` (`construct.py:52`) renders it.
`packet.public_operations` (`packet.py:116`) advertises bare
`reduce_software`, bare `reduce_graph`, and `oracle.query`.
`packet.entry_rules` (`packet.py:77`) derives from
`method_exec.entry_contract`, so the prompt and the execution gate read one
contract object. The driver template injects the `reducers` module into the
child namespace (`method_exec.py:106`) without binding the bare names. That
gap is the A1 contract mismatch. Phase A owns the fix. This report only
records the two sides.

### Construction

`_run_boundary` takes the development branch (`trajectory.py:453-542`) and
calls `construct.construct_method` (`construct.py:185`). At most two lineages
per trajectory, one init plus one repair attempt each. Each attempt is one
broker `model-inference` operation
(`ad01-<episode>-construct-l<lineage>-<init|repair>`) with settled-receipt
reuse (`construct._settled_text:66`). Responses parse through
`packet.parse_construction_response`, gate through
`method_exec.verify_member`, execute through
`run_member_out_of_process` under a `-validate-l` operation id, and check
through `trajectory._check`. `ConstructionFailed` becomes a `rejected`
episode with its calls and queries counted (`trajectory.py:508-523`).
Both domains run the same loop. Only the task bytes differ.

### Diagnostics

`run_diagnostic` (`trajectory.py:1035`) dispatches on
`_DIAGNOSTICS = {"software": _resolve_sw, "graph": _resolve_gr}`
(`trajectory.py:39`). Software compares diagnostic against nondiagnostic
seed verdicts (`controls.diagnostic_resolves`, via `_resolve_sw:21`).
Graph compares seed against novel ordering under a query budget
(`controls.novel_order_unproductive`, via `_resolve_gr:29`). Family mismatch
between the admitted diagnostic and the task family is refused twice, once
in `_run_boundary` (`trajectory.py:394-406`) and once in `run_diagnostic`.
The observation record carries `observation_id`, `basis_references` with a
`grounded` flag, verdict, queries, and detail.

### Revision

Revision today means bounded correction, not method revision. Inside one
decision, `DecisionConsumer.decide` retries the proposer up to
`MAX_CORRECTIONS = 2`, journaling each failure as a `correction`
observation and pinning the refused target (`agenda_policy.py:197-263`).
Inside construction, the repair attempt gets the prior failure verbatim
(`construct.py:211`). No path revises an already retained repertoire member.
`dev_episode(break_candidate=True)` is a test probe that deliberately
breaks a candidate (`trajectory.py:1002`). It is not a revision mechanism.

### Retention

`dev_episode` (`trajectory.py:966`) retains when the check verdict is
`preserved` and the candidate is strictly smaller than the incumbent
(`trajectory.py:1017-1032`). The episode stores executable bytes, entry
name, source digest, scope family, and lineage. `run_campaign` appends
retained executables to in-memory experience (`trajectory.py:691-693`).
`freeze_repertoire` (`trajectory.py:719`) writes members to a JSON file.
`load_repertoire` (`trajectory.py:733`) refuses members whose bytes mismatch
their digest. The live study retained nothing. All six frozen repertoires
hold zero members (`evidence_inv01_live/README.md`).

### Later use

`run_use` (`trajectory.py:783`) loads a frozen repertoire and runs each use
task once. `_select_member` (`trajectory.py:756`) returns the first member
whose scope family matches. It never compares members and never looks
across families. Seeds run in process (`seeds.run_seed`). Acquired members
run out of process under `ad01-<cid>-use-<task>`
(`trajectory.py:797-800`). Member failure falls back to
`controls.incumbent` with a recorded reason (`trajectory.py:808-837`).
Every record carries freeze identity, verdict, measures, executed source,
and operation ids. `checker.verify_use_records` and the frozen benefit rule
(`benefit.py`) judge the records, not the runner.

### Crash recovery

`resume_campaign` (`trajectory.py:940`) re-runs `run_campaign` on the same
DSN and campaign id. Settled boundaries replay from `attempt_observations`
without duplicate spend (`run_campaign:648-671`). A recorded `decision`
without a `boundary` resumes through the accepted path instead of
re-deciding (`_run_boundary:338-340`). Settled model receipts are reused by
operation id in the learner (`learner._settled_text:132`) and in
construction (`construct._settled_text:66`). Settled sandbox receipts are
reused the same way (`method_exec.py:220-225`). The diagnostic checkpoint in
`study_phases` restores observation, remaining budgets, and counters
(`trajectory._diagnostic_checkpoint:286`). Unresolved operations export as
`unknown_exposure` liabilities (`records.py:395-402`).

### Export

`records.export_campaign` (`records.py:258`) emits one transition per
boundary. Each transition holds a reconstructed packet with its digest, a
proposal derived from the recorded decision
(`_proposal_from_decision:198`), operations, receipts, raw model responses,
retained bytes with recomputed digests, measured and unknown costs, and
spend. The run id binds campaign, database, freeze digest, model, and
packet version (`records.py:403-407`). `verify_byte_chain`, `recompute_accounting`,
`recompute_from_corpus`, `verify_campaign`, and `verify_study`
(`records.py:447,551,579,626,769`) check the export without new execution.

### Settlement facilities actually used by AD01

AD01 uses `broker` (ensure, dispatch, read), `store` (commitment,
acquire, observations, subdivide, prepare), `authority` (authorize, bind,
phase notes), `db` (connections), `gateway` (seam for live, recording, and
fake adapters), `launcher_local` (child profile), `common` (commands,
digests), and `loop.ResourceEnvelope.sequence_construction_allowance`.
AD01 does not call settlement `context` packet builders. Those serve the
older `team.py` (`team.py:616,633,1229`) and `development.py`
(`development.py:542,554`) runtimes only. AD01 does not call settlement
`artifacts.py` either. No `artifacts` import exists under
`experiments/ad01/`. Any alternative claiming reuse of context or artifact
facilities must first wire AD01 to them. Today that reuse is asserted, not
implemented.

## 3. Existing replay and context implementations

Replay is `doubles.check_replay_prefix` (`experiments/doubles.py:353`),
reached through `replay_export` (`doubles.py:339`) and the `replay`
subcommand. A probe names a transition index, a packet digest, an action,
versions, and observations. Supported requires exact packet digest,
matching target and instrument, matching version fields (model,
packet_version, protocol, profile, freeze_digest), byte-identical
code inputs by digest, exact dependency order (a reorder returns
`unsupported`, specifically `reordered-dependents`), equal requested
resources, and observations drawn only from the recorded prefix pool (a
future observation returns `hidden-future` unsupported). Anything else
returns `refused` for malformed probes. Supported probes return recorded
results verbatim with unknowns marked unknown. The checker never borrows
an unrelated outcome and never scores unknown as failure. This already
satisfies the INVESTIGATION-01 replay boundary. Its gap is coverage, not
semantics. With zero retained members and ten construction receipts, most
interesting probes are honestly unsupported.

Context materialization exists twice and the two do not connect. AD01
`packet.py` builds decision and construction packets from live experience
plus a seed observation, with no database read. Settlement `context.py`
builds versioned packets from stored state with claim, artifact, and
applicability checks (`context.py:918 build_packet`, `:1016
bind_packet_invocation`, `:1080 revalidate_packet`), but only `team.py`
and `development.py` call it. A replacement proposal must state which of
the two survives. Keeping both keeps two packet truths.

## 4. Alternatives

### Alternative 1: consolidate in the current consumer and driver

Concrete usage. A fresh process runs `ad01-traj run --world 0 --arm I`
against an authorized DSN. `run_campaign` iterates the schedule.
`_run_boundary` builds the decision packet, `DecisionConsumer.decide`
proposes through the model policy and admits through `admit_investigation`,
the diagnostic runs, `construct_method` builds and checks a method, the
boundary publishes, the repertoire freezes, and `ad01-traj use` runs the
frozen bytes on protected tasks. Consolidation moves the whole-episode
bookkeeping that now sprawls across `_run_boundary` (seen packet,
correction allowance, checkpoint restore, construction caps, episode
assembly) into `DecisionConsumer` methods with named investigation state,
and moves the seq-indexed resume logic (`settled` replay plus `pending`
accept) behind one `reconcile` function. The CLI, operation id scheme, and
tables stay.

Ownership. `experiments/ad01/trajectory.py` plus `agenda_policy.py` own
investigation state transitions. `packet.py` owns both packet shapes.
`construct.py` and `method_exec.py` own construction and execution.
`records.py` plus `doubles.py` own export and replay. Settlement keeps
authority, admission, and evidence. Domain modules (`worlds`, `seeds`,
`controls`, `reducers`, `checkers`) become instruments behind the
`_family` dispatch. Experiments supply conditions and judges
(`benefit.py`, `checker.py`, rotation schedules).

Migration cost. Small to medium. Move code inside `experiments/ad01/`.
No table migration. No pending-work migration, because the
decision/boundary/observation kinds keep their meanings. Delete
`next_decision` or wire it as the stop rule so one helper stops lying
about being the decider. Callers (`cli.py`, `scripts/inv01_study.py`,
`run_c3_qualification.py`, the B1 and C3 tests) keep working. The risk is
that whole-episode assumptions survive: the fixed task list, seq-indexed
operation ids, `DEV_EPISODE_CAP` per trajectory, and in-memory experience
rebuilt from settled rows on every resume.

### Alternative 2: extract an action-level persistent investigation core

Concrete usage. A caller opens an investigation once and receives an
investigation id that outlives campaigns. It proposes actions of kinds
`diagnostic`, `construct`, `use`, `revise`, and `stop`. The core records
each accepted action before execution, executes through broker operations,
publishes observations, and tracks pending actions across processes. A
fresh process calls `resume(investigation_id)` and reconciles pending
actions instead of re-running a campaign loop. `ad01-traj run` becomes a
thin client that maps worlds and schedules onto core actions. The same
core serves a future mathematics instrument that registers its own task
loader, oracle, and checker behind the versioned instrument contract.

Ownership. A new core module, likely `src/settlement/investigation.py` or
`experiments/ad01/investigation.py`, owns the investigation record,
pending-action lifecycle, revision lineage, and the reconcile path. The
broker and store keep authority and evidence exactly as today. The
consumer becomes one policy over the core action contract, next to the
baseline. Experiments become clients. They supply worlds, treatments, and
judges, and they lose the right to implement their own grants, loops, or
accounting, per INVESTIGATION-01 section "One resource and experience
path".

Migration cost. Medium to large. Move `record_decision`,
`_publish_boundary`, `_read_campaign`, the pending-accepted path, the
diagnostic checkpoint, and the consumer into the core. Redefine pending
work: today's `decision`-without-`boundary` rows and `study_phases`
checkpoints migrate to pending-action rows or stay readable through a
compatibility view. Every in-flight campaign migrates or drains first.
`cli.py`, `scripts/inv01_study.py`, and `run_c3_qualification.py` change
to the client interface. `packet.py` merges with settlement `context.py`
or one of them is deleted. The risk is building a second runtime beside
the broker and store, which already give idempotent commands and receipt
reuse. The core must prove it adds lifecycle semantics the store lacks,
not a second copy of them.

## 5. Evaluation on the six criteria

Branching. Under alternative 1, a branch is a new campaign id plus a
copied schedule. Cheap, but branches share nothing and cannot rejoin.
Seq-indexed operation ids collide in meaning across branches because only
the campaign prefix differs. Under alternative 2, a branch pins starting
knowledge, policy, and dependencies as one record, which matches the
Branch and replay contract directly. Advantage: alternative 2, but only
matters once something actually branches. Nothing branches today.

Context loss. Under alternative 1, a crash loses only uncheckpointed
work. Settled boundaries, decisions, corrections, and receipts all
persist, and resume rebuilds experience from them. The in-memory
`retained` list is the weak point. It rebuilds from settled episodes,
which works, but only because episodes embed executables. Under
alternative 2, pending actions are first-class rows, so a crash between
acceptance and execution reconciles instead of replaying the loop. The
practical gap is narrow. Both survive the kills that INVESTIGATION-01
qualification section 3 names. Advantage: slight, alternative 2.

Policy replacement. Both expose the same seam. The consumer already takes
`proposer` and `admit` as constructor arguments, and the I/R arms already
differ only in curriculum while sharing the pipeline. Swapping the model
policy for the baseline changes actual admitted behavior through the real
path today. Neither alternative improves this. The missing piece is a
richer executable procedure interface, which is a contract design task,
not an ownership task. Advantage: neither. Do not let this criterion
decide.

Domain dependence. Both keep domain code behind `_family` dispatch,
versioned oracles, and per-family checkers. Alternative 1 keeps the
dispatch table in the driver. Alternative 2 moves it behind an instrument
registry. The registry only pays off with a third instrument. The brief
forbids adding a domain to claim generality. Advantage: alternative 1
until a real third instrument exists.

State duplication. Alternative 1 keeps one packet builder, one resume
path, and one replay checker, and deletes the dead decider. But it leaves
settlement `context.py` and AD01 `packet.py` as parallel packet truths,
and `team.py`/`development.py` as parallel drivers. Alternative 2 must
merge or delete one packet implementation to earn its name. A thin core
over unchanged duplicate loops is not consolidation, per the brief.
Advantage: alternative 2 if it deletes, alternative 1 if alternative 2
only wraps. Judge the delivered diff, not the diagram.

Interface depth. Count the layers a probe crosses. Today a construction
goes CLI to campaign to boundary to consumer to proposer to broker to
gateway, then back through parser, gate, child driver, oracle socket,
checker, episode, publish, and export. That is already deep. Alternative 1
adds no layer. Alternative 2 adds a core layer between experiments and the
broker. The added layer is justified only if pending-action reconcile
replaces logic in several callers rather than adding to it. Advantage:
alternative 1 by default. Alternative 2 must show deleted call sites.

## 6. Trajectory walk under each alternative

The same nine steps, with what the system does without a new
human-written controller between steps.

Limitation. The model policy proposes a diagnostic on a software task
whose seed verdicts disagree. Under both, `model_propose` emits the
action and `admit_investigation` admits it on recorded basis references.

Investigation. Under both, the diagnostic runs (`_resolve_sw`) and its
observation joins experience. Under alternative 2 the observation also
closes a pending `diagnostic` action row. Same visible behavior.

Construct and check. The policy proposes development. Under both,
`construct_method` spends init and repair attempts through broker
operations, gates and executes out of process, and checks preservation.
A `NameError` on bare helper names fails today at execution with the
receipt kept. That failure mode is Phase A1 input.

Retain or reject. A preserved, smaller candidate retains with bytes,
digest, and lineage. Anything else rejects with stage and reason. Under
both, the boundary publishes and the loop continues. No human intervenes.

Fresh use. A new process loads the frozen repertoire and runs protected
tasks. `_select_member` picks by family. Under both, use records carry
executed source and operation ids, and the benefit rule judges arms.

Scope failure. A retained software method applied to a graph task is
never selected, because selection matches scope family. A subtler scope
failure, a method that passes dev tasks but fails protected tasks with
the same family, records failed use records and stops there. Under both,
nothing proposes a revision today. This is the largest functional gap in
either alternative. Both must add a `revise` trigger from failed use.

Revision proposal. Under alternative 1, a new boundary proposes
development on the failed task with the failure as `prior_failure`, and
construction produces a candidate with a lineage link to the old member.
Under alternative 2, the failed use opens a pending `revise` action
pinned to the old member digest, and the same construction path fills it.
Alternative 2 records the intent before the work. Alternative 1
reconstructs it from packets after the fact.

Independent assessment. Under both, the revised bytes execute out of
process, check against the frozen oracle, and run fresh use tasks under
the same benefit rule. The judge, visibility rules, and resource ceiling
do not change with the proposal, satisfying the Assessment and retention
contract.

Keep or reject. A preserved improvement on the failed task retains and
supersedes the old member for its scope. Otherwise the old member stays
and the attempt records as rejected lineage. Under alternative 2 the
pending `revise` action closes either way. Under alternative 1 the
boundary row is the only trace. The difference is auditability of intent,
not capability.

## 7. Preliminary pick, pending Phase A

Preliminary pick: alternative 1, consolidate in the current
consumer and driver, with two mandatory deletions and one addition.

Reasons, each tied to source. The consumer gate already separates policy
from mechanism. `DecisionConsumer` takes proposer and admit as arguments
(`agenda_policy.py:92-97`), the model and baseline policies already share
the pipeline, and I/R qualification differs only in curriculum selection
(INVESTIGATION-01 section "Decision policies and replay boundary"). The
admission and execution path already persists acceptance before effects
(`record_decision` before `_run_boundary` work), resumes pending work
without duplicate spend, and reuses settled receipts by operation id
across learner, construction, and sandbox calls. The replay checker
already enforces the exact boundary the brief demands: digest, versions,
code bytes, dependency order, resources, and prefix-only observations
(`doubles.py:353-460`). These are the guarantees the direction note says
to keep, and they live in the driver already. Alternative 2 rehouses them
without a demonstrated missing capability. No trajectory has needed to
branch, no crash has lost committed work, and no third instrument exists
to justify the registry. The concrete defects are local: a dead decider
(`next_decision`), a prompt-to-executor namespace gap (Phase A1), an
unconnected settlement context facility, and no revise trigger from
failed use. Alternative 1 fixes each where it lives. The required
addition is the revise trigger plus lineage between superseded members.
The required deletions are `next_decision` (or its promotion to the real
stop rule) and one of the two packet builders.

What Phase A findings could change the pick. If A2 shows archived
failures need cross-boundary retries or branching that seq-indexed
operation ids cannot express, the pending-action core of alternative 2
becomes necessary rather than tidy. If the A3 feasibility probes show a
substituted policy cannot change admitted behavior through the consumer
path, the consumer is the wrong consolidation point. If Phase A adds new
authority, attempt, or lineage tables to close stage 8, alternative 2 may
already be half built, and finishing it beats maintaining two schemes.
If the corrected contract moves callable ownership into the executor in a
way that turns domain modules into true instruments with registered
versions, the registry argument strengthens. Until one of these shows in
evidence, the smaller consolidation holds. This pick is preliminary and
waits on the merged Phase A findings.
