## Corrected E0 interpretation

E0 proves that live model bytes reached retention only. It does not prove model acquisition, binding, inheritance, or utility. The historical `bound` labels in the worker report are as-run projections and are superseded by the acquisition review. A retained digest is not an active binding. A post-restart load is not an invoked descendant. A model label is not model-response provenance.

The archived E0 result remains useful for a narrower claim. It records one live response, a retained candidate, and the failure of the subsequent path to establish the stronger claims. The correction belongs in the projection and completion language, not in a rewrite of the archived evidence.

## E3 eligibility gate

E3 is unavailable unless a genuinely eligible model revision exists. All four conditions are required.

| Condition | Required evidence | Ineligible shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Model-response provenance | The executable bytes trace to the exact model response through its response digest, construction record, validation, and package manifest. | A fixed authored menu, system-selected authored source, or an `origin` label. |
| Exact-digest binding | The candidate package digest and executable digest identify the same revision, and the durable active package digest equals that candidate digest. | Retention, a parent digest, or a reported bound digest that differs from the active digest. |
| Durable post-restart child citation | After process replacement, an independently checked child invocation cites the same bound executable digest in durable evidence. | A marker, a restart that only reloads state, or a resumed round that invokes another package. |
| Usable result | The candidate produces a valid executable result under the declared parsing, ABI, behavior, and assessment protocol. | A retained package with no usable result or a no-op candidate. |

Retained-only, digest-mismatched, and authored or control-derived bytes are ineligible. A no-op candidate can be rejected honestly. E3 must not run an empty panel. It becomes eligible only after all four conditions are present and independently checkable.
