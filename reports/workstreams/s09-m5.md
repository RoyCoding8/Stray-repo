## Decision

The pilot is a skeleton on the M1 tip. It freezes the N5 study spec,
runs four development episodes, constructs P1 with permitted-experience
differences recorded, and assesses twelve episodes with two sealed use
tasks each through the public trajectory entry. Doubles sit only at
the gateway seam. The 28-prefix replay stays a conformance check. No
live inference ran.

P2 construction fails its repair in doubles mode, so P2 ships as an
unavailable arm with incumbent fallback and a narrower result. That
outcome exercises the rule that missing candidates stay unavailable
arms and never become rebranded baselines.
