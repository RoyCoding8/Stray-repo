### The two architecture-relevant bottlenecks

The assignment permits no more than three. The analysis concludes **two**, and
the cap is a maximum rather than a target. The third this ledger previously
named, the output budget, was measured and falsified by B17; it is recorded
above as a corrected inference, not as a bottleneck. Full argument in
[bottlenecks.md](workstreams/bottlenecks.md).

1. **The system has no independent unit for any learning claim.** Every
   disposition above acquisition rests on comparing two arms, and the design
   supplies one independent unit of variation per structure, so a contrast is
   n=1 however many rows it runs. The cluster rule is `(family, template)` and
   six clusters are required. From the panel census: **exactly 3 of 14 panels
   are powered, all graph-only**; all **7 software panels are unpowered** at a
   maximum of 4 against 6. The Boolean structure offers **exactly 1**
   hypothesis class against 6 — all 72 tasks carry a byte-identical
   `hypothesis_class` while carrying 72 distinct hidden truth tables. The tasks
   differ; the class does not, and it is a published constant carried by
   construction, so no panel choice moves it. Going to six units needs six
   different generators.
   The SWE side's `powered: True` rests on
   `len(set(DEV_TEMPLATES) | set(HELD_OUT_TEMPLATES))` at
   `twodomain.py:119-126`, which unions dev and held-out to reach the
   threshold. Per split that is **3 or 6 against 6 required**: dev is
   unpowered, held-out is exactly at threshold with zero margin. Counting both
   splits together clears the bar through the very census the split exists to
   prevent. The crossing then spends 2 dev episodes and 1 held-out, which is
   the honest ratio and a 3-unit dev panel.
2. **The chain's effect identity was synthesized, so two arrows were bound by
   position rather than by cause — now repaired, and recorded here as the
   architecture lesson rather than as an open defect.** It ranked second on
   blocking power and first on severity. X4b closed it; what survives is that
   the proof the batch relied on was the existence of rows, and the causal
   binding was not tested by anything, so it will keep proving the weaker
   thing until something reads the join.

**What is not a bottleneck, stated so it is not re-argued.** Fixed defects: the
freeze gap, the concurrent-amend lock, and the migration-count and menu-test
migrations are all repaired. Unfinished work: `c4-live` and `b5-sealed-state`
never ran, which bounds what a campaign measured and says nothing about the
design. Free-route instability: B12's 502s and timeout are external-dependency
observations about one route.
