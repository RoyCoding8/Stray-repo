# The conclusion in this directory is retracted

The mechanism claim in `RESULT.md` here — that experience steers the
diagnostic the model proposes — **does not survive challenge**. It is kept
unedited as the record of what was claimed and when, and this notice sits
with it so it cannot be read as a live result.

What refuted it: a 2×2 across both target families and both experience
conditions, then a six-cell replication. The `diagnostic` field echoes the
most recent family token in context and does so **identically with and
without experience** — six for six on the family shown, and the `none` arms
agree with the `relevant` ones because the charter carries the family either
way. Every contrast that read a difference in this field was reading an echo.

Full reasoning and data: `../inv_r1_e2_challenge/RESULT.md`.
